The hands behind every pair

It started with one pair, made for herself.

On a trip to Gorkha Bazaar, Sushma bought a pair of slippers so pretty she wanted to learn to make them. Her aunt taught her the basics in a single day. Back home she made two pairs, then a few more for friends and family, reinvesting every rupee into more material.

Eight years later, Orange makes around 300 pairs a month, sold across Nepal and carried by Nepalis abroad to Japan, Korea, Dubai, Australia and Croatia. Raised by her mother, Sushma has supported her family with this work since finishing +2.

“No work is big or small. But every work must be done honestly.”“काम सानो-ठूलो हुँदैन। तर हरेक काम इमानदार भएर गर्नुपर्छ।”Sushma Shrestha, to Routine of Nepal Banda

From sole to stitch

How a pair is made

No factory line. Sushma cuts on one day, then glues, stitches and packs the next, about 20 pairs every two days, from early morning until late at night.

Cut

काट्ने

Soles and uppers are traced and cut by hand from velvet, fabric and leather bought in local markets.

Glue & shape

टाँस्ने

Uppers are glued to the sole and shaped over a foot last, so the pair holds its form.

Stitch & finish

सिउने

Every seam is stitched, then the piping, zari or stones are added by hand.

Pack & send

प्याक गर्ने

Each pair is checked, packed in our orange-printed bag and sent off anywhere in Nepal.
