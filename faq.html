Questions, answered

Can't find what you need? Message us on WhatsApp or call 980-2051265.

Ordering

How do I order?
Choose your slippers, colour and size, add them to your bag, then press Place order. Fill in your delivery details and tap Send order on WhatsApp. Your order arrives in our chat, already written out. We reply to confirm everything before we send it.
Can I pay online?
Not on the website yet. After you send your order, we'll confirm the total (including delivery) and agree on how you'd like to pay in the chat.
I don't use WhatsApp. Can I still order?
Of course. On the last step, tap Copy order text and paste it into Facebook Messenger, Viber or Instagram, or just call us on 980-2051265.
Can you make a pair to my size or design?
Yes. Custom orders are a big part of what we do. Send us your foot length (see the size guide), the design or a photo of what you like, and the colour. We'll tell you the price and how long it will take.
How long does it take?
Pairs we have ready go out after we confirm your order. Made-to-order and custom pairs take a few extra days, and longer in Dashain, Tihar and wedding season, so order early. We'll always tell you the expected date before you pay.

Delivery

Where do you deliver?
All over Nepal, from Pokhara and Kathmandu to Butwal, Biratnagar, Bharatpur and beyond, through our courier partner.
How much is delivery?
It depends on where you are and how many pairs you order. We confirm the exact charge in the chat before sending, so there are no surprises.
Do you send abroad?
Our slippers have already travelled to Japan, Korea, Dubai, Australia and Croatia. Message us with your country and we'll find the best way to get them to you.

Returns and care

Can I return or exchange?
Because every pair is handmade, often to order, we don't offer returns or exchanges. Please measure using our size guide and ask us if you're unsure. We're happy to help you choose.
How do I look after velvet and zari slippers?
Brush velvet with a soft, dry brush or cloth. Keep zari, beads and stones away from water and perfume. If they get damp, let them dry in the shade, not direct sun. Store them in their bag so the velvet doesn't get crushed.
Will my pair look exactly like the photo?
Very close, but each pair is made by hand, so embroidery placement, print patterns and colour shades can vary a little. That's part of the charm.
