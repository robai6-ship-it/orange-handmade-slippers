Find your size

Handmade pairs can't be exchanged, so a minute with a ruler now saves a disappointment later. Between sizes? We'll make yours to measure.

How to measure

  1. Stand on a sheet of paper with your heel against a wall, in the evening when feet are a little bigger.
  2. Mark the tip of your longest toe, then measure from the wall to the mark in centimetres.
  3. Measure both feet and use the longer one. Find it in the table.
  4. Wide feet or between two sizes? Pick the bigger one, or write your foot length in the order notes and we'll make the pair to fit.
Not sure? Send a photo of your foot on the measured paper to us on WhatsApp and we'll tell you your size.
