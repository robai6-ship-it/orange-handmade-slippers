What you'll learn

From a flat sheet to a finished pair

  • Choosing materials
    Soles, velvet, fabric, leather and glue, and where to buy them.
  • Measuring and cutting
    Tracing patterns for each size and cutting soles and uppers cleanly.
  • Gluing and shaping on a last
    Getting a pair that holds its shape and fits well.
  • Stitching and finishing
    Machine stitching, piping, and decorating with zari and stones.
  • Packing and selling
    Pricing, packing and how we take orders on social media.

Good to know

  • Hands-on, in our workshop, in small groups
  • No experience needed, just patience and interest
  • Every graduate receives a certificate
  • Open to everyone; we especially welcome women who want their own income
